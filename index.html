Services

Tanker booking on the Water Board network

PaaniLink is the hydrant tanker desk: request water, schedule a slot, and keep one status from the hydrant to your address.

Commercial delivery

Households and businesses book 1,000, 3,000 or 6,000 gallon tankers at the published rate. No officer approval.

Subsidised delivery

Verified households, hospitals and mosques pay the subsidised rate. Each request is reviewed by the zone officer.

Group request

One form for several neighbouring addresses or an institution, each with its own tanker size on the same date.

Track delivery

Use your PL reference to see filling, dispatch and delivery without signing in.

How it works

How a booking is handled

  1. 1
    You choose a hydrant in your zone, a tanker size, and a delivery date within operating hours.
  2. 2
    Commercial requests go to the hydrant desk. Subsidised requests wait for the zone officer.
  3. 3
    The hydrant operator assigns a tanker and driver, records filling against the daily quota, then departure.
  4. 4
    The driver marks arrival and delivery. You confirm the drop or open a dispute.

Help

Questions

How do I book?

Sign in or register, then choose hydrant, capacity and a delivery slot. The charge is shown before you submit.

When can I cancel?

You can cancel until a tanker is assigned. After dispatch, contact the zone office from the booking record.

How do I report a short fill or no-show?

When the driver marks delivered, open the booking and choose Report / dispute. The zone officer records the outcome.
